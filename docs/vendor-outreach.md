# Vendor Outreach — Templates & Talking Points

For recruiting water suppliers into the eight Karachi zones FlowX doesn't cover
yet: DHA, Federal B Area, Gulberg, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Korangi, North Karachi,
PECHS and Saddar.

Every claim below is checked against what the system actually does. Where a
number is configurable, it says so.

---

## 0. Read this before you send anything

**Do not promise order volume.** FlowX has no live order history to speak of.
The honest pitch is *"be first in your area"*, not *"we'll send you 50 orders a
week"* — a supplier who signs up expecting volume and gets three orders churns
angry, and tells other suppliers. Under-promising costs you nothing here.

Three things that are true today and worth leading with:

- Joining is free, with no monthly fee and no exclusivity — they can keep every
  existing customer and walk away whenever they like.
- Their area probably has **no** FlowX vendor, so every order placed there would
  be theirs.
- They keep **80%** of product value (20% commission, adjustable by admin).

Two things to be straight about, because they'll find out anyway:

- **Approval is not instant.** Admin approval *and* KYC both have to clear.
- **There is no automatic SMS on approval yet** — no SMS gateway is configured,
  so someone has to phone them. Promise a call, not a text.

## 3. If they don't reply

One follow-up. Not more — a second chase reads as desperation and kills the
relationship for later.

> Assalam-o-Alaikum {NAME} — just following up on {AREA}. No rush from my side.
> If it's not for you that's completely fine, just let me know and I won't
> disturb you again. If you'd rather talk than type, tell me a good time and
> I'll call.

Roman Urdu:

> Assalam-o-Alaikum {NAME} — {AREA} wali baat ke silsilay mein yaad dahani. Koi
> jaldi nahi. Agar aap ki dilchaspi nahi to bilkul theek, bas bata dijiye ga,
> main dobara zehmat nahi doon ga. Baat karni ho to waqt bata dein, call kar
> leta hoon.

## 4. On the phone — what to actually say

Suppliers decide on the phone, not over WhatsApp. Keep it to four beats:

1. **Who you are and why them** — "You cover {AREA}. Nobody on FlowX does."
2. **What changes for them** — orders come in already placed and paid or
   confirmed; no haggling, no chasing calls.
3. **What it costs** — nothing to join, 20% of order value, no lock-in.
4. **One ask** — "Can I sign you up now while we're on the call?" Do it with
   them on the phone; a link sent for later gets forgotten.

Let them talk. A supplier's first question tells you what they actually care
about — usually money, sometimes control.

## 5. Objections you will hear

**"What's the catch? Why is it free?"**
> No catch. We make money only when you make money — 20% of orders we send you.
> If we send you nothing, we earn nothing.

**"20% is too much."**
> Compare it to what you spend now finding a new customer. These are orders you
> wouldn't otherwise have, and you don't pay anything for the ones you don't
> take.
*(If you decide to negotiate: commission is adjustable per vendor and per
product in the admin panel. Agree the number before they sign, and set it
straight away.)*

**"When do I get my money?"**
> Order value lands in your wallet the moment you mark it delivered. Cash you
> collect on COD is tracked against your account and settled with us — you'll
> see every order, every deduction, itemised in the app.

**"I'm busy. What if I miss an order?"**
> It goes to the next vendor after 90 seconds — no penalty, nothing owed. And if
> you're closed for the day, flip the Closed switch and you'll stop being
> offered anything at all.

**"Do I have to give up my own customers?"**
> No. Nothing about FlowX is exclusive. Your customers stay yours; this is extra
> on top.

**"Why do you need my CNIC?"**
> You'll be handling customers' cash and going to their homes. Every vendor is
> verified, which is exactly why customers trust ordering through us at all.

## 8. Where to find suppliers

Not a list of leads — you know Karachi better than any list would. But the
places worth working, roughly in order of how warm the lead is:

- **Existing water shops in the target area.** Walk in. They already have the
  bottles, the stock and the delivery bike; FlowX is just more orders.
- **Suppliers who already deliver to homes** in nearby covered areas and might
  extend one zone over.
- **Your two current vendors.** Ask them directly who they know in DHA or
  Gulshan. A referral from a vendor who is already earning is the strongest
  introduction you will get — and if that channel works, tell me and I'll build
  the vendor referral scheme so it's tracked and rewarded automatically.
- **Water filtration plant operators**, who often want a retail channel.

Check `/admin/zones` before each round of outreach — the **Turned away (30d)**
column tells you which uncovered area has real customers waiting, and that's the
one to work first.
